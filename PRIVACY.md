# Star Nav — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Star Nav does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. It has no
accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works fully offline.

## What the app uses, and where it stays

- **Location (optional).** Your approximate location is used on your device to work out which
  part of the sky is above you and when objects rise and set. It is never sent to us or to any
  third party. To show a place name, iOS may look up your location using Apple's own
  geocoding service, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You
  can deny location access and type a location in Settings instead.
- **Motion sensors.** Readings from the compass, gyroscope and accelerometer are used, on your
  device and only while the app is open, to follow where you point your phone. They are not
  stored or transmitted.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (layers, night mode, label size, a manually entered location)
  are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.
